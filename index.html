How I work
“Each problem has its own way.”From my portfolio deck

Big, unclear problems get the full Triple Diamond: research and business needs first, then design decisions, then build and test.

Small, annoying ones get Lean UX: watch people, fix the smallest thing, check it with them, repeat.

Teams new to UX get a workshop before anything else.

My own products I build early and let customers tell me what's wrong.
